# VegaBar privacy policy

VegaBar collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere.

- **No network.** VegaBar makes no internet connections. There is no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, account or cloud service.
- **Bluetooth.** VegaBar uses Bluetooth only to find, pair with and control your Zhiyun lights.
- **Camera.** If you turn on the camera automation, VegaBar asks macOS whether any camera is in use, so it can turn your light on during calls. It never opens a camera, records anything or sees the picture.
- **What stays on your Mac.** Your settings and presets, the pairing keys for your lights, and, only if you turn it on, a diagnostic log of Bluetooth frames. These stay on your Mac in VegaBar's own folders. Uninstalling VegaBar and deleting those folders removes them.
- **Logs.** VegaBar writes diagnostic messages to the macOS system log on your Mac. They describe your lights, never you, and leave your Mac only if you share them yourself.

VegaBar is open source, so you can check all of this in the code.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
